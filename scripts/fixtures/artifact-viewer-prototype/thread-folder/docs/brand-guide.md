# Tidewater brand guide

Tidewater is a small-batch coffee roaster on the harbour. This guide keeps the website, packaging and social posts consistent.

## Voice

- Warm, plain and specific. Name the farm, the roast date and the price we paid.
- Short sentences. No "artisanal", no "elevated".
- Speak to one person, not "coffee lovers".

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sea | `#0f6e6a` | Primary actions, links |
| Sand | `#f6efe4` | Page backgrounds |
| Ember | `#d9783b` | Highlights, featured plan |
| Ink | `#1d2a2a` | Body text |

## Logo

The mark is two waves inside a circle. Keep clear space equal to one wave height on every side. Never stretch it, recolour it or put it on a busy photo.

## Photography

Natural light, real cups, real hands. Avoid stock photos of latte art.

## Packaging

Every bag carries: origin, farm, roast date, tasting notes and the price per kilo paid to the grower.
