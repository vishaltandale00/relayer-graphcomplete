# Live answers preserve sealed initial input

Accepted product direction on 2026-10-09: the simulated user may answer a current-layer question while the same completion runs. Keep its initial CompletionContract immutable and record supplemental answers separately, rather than stopping execution and starting another completion. GraphComplete owns answer acceptance and scoped reads; the provider owns waiting and consumption. The external boundary remains `complete(inputGraph)`.

An explicit Answer snapshots the staged value for an ordinary Input question in the exact displayed current Layer, root human completion, execution attempt and authority generation. The graph validates the accepted action, source membership, type/options and current revision in the same transaction as appending its immutable receipt. Invoke-bound inputs, semantic children, imported records, foreign scopes and stale generations are unavailable in this first slice. One answer belongs to one exact Layer/action occurrence; re-answering conflicts. Exact-key retries recover the original receipt even after Return or Stop. A new answer loses admission if settlement commits first. Accepted receipt means delivery, not model incorporation.

Product reads graph-owned receipts for reconciliation and reopening, including layer-specific historical receipts after Current advances. Historical Delivered questions keep their accepted value and do not enter the ordinary composer. Failed receipt lookup is unavailable and grants no new input authority. Unsent stages do not reach the candidate; ambiguous requests retain their frozen key/value and are never automatically replayed or restored into the ordinary Send draft. The candidate reads supplemental answers through its existing completion capability, with an ordered cursor and bounded provider-authored waiting. There is no inference restart, automatic resume, new child invocation or scheduler. Initial contract, accepted graph history, invocation arguments and completion admission counts stay unchanged.

Current-native actor setups may choose a visible control action or keep observing, under the existing combined action budget and deadline. Working observations and answer intents/receipts remain chronological evidence. Historical settled-only setups retain their original behavior. PRD §13.2.3 owns this contract; ADR 0008 Input's draft privacy and ADR 0015's sealed initial authority remain in force.
